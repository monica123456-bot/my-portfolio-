# AI Prompt Log

1. Prompt Used

I am currently in my second year studying Computer Information Systems at university, and I am creating a simple personal portfolio webpage to introduce myself and my interests.

Help me write an About Me section that I can use on the webpage.

Please:

- Keep it between 60 and 80 words.
- Make it sound natural, confident, and friendly.
- Write it from the perspective of a university student who is still learning and developing her skills.
- Include my interest in web development and technology.
- Focus on my current interests and learning rather than claiming achievements or qualifications I have not mentioned.
- Avoid complicated or overly formal language.

Return only the About Me paragraph.

 1. AI Raw Output

I am a second-year Computer Information Systems student with a growing passion for technology and web development. I enjoy learning how websites are built and exploring new tools that help create useful and engaging digital experiences. As I continue developing my skills, I am excited to take on new challenges, learn from every project, and build a strong foundation for a future career in the technology field.

 1. Final Edited Version

I am a second-year Computer Information Systems student with an interest in technology and web development. I enjoy learning how websites are created and exploring different tools as I develop my skills. I am currently improving my knowledge of programming and information systems through my studies and practical projects. I look forward to learning more, taking on new challenges, and gaining more experience in the technology field.

 1. Reflection

I edited the AI-generated paragraph to make it sound simpler and more natural. I removed some formal phrases and changed the wording to better reflect my current experience as a student who is still learning and developing my skills.
